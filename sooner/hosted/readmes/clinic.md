# Sooner Clinic Agent

Serves one clinic on Sooner. Answers appointment requests and bookings from the Sooner matchmaker.
The clinic owner can manage slots by chatting here:

- `add slot 2026-10-28T10:00 150`
- `list slots`
- `cancel booking <slot_id>`: frees the slot and re-offers it to waiting patients
- `remove slot <slot_id>`

_Demo: clinics and payments are simulated._
